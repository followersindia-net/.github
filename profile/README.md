## ❓ What Is an SMM Panel?

**SMM** stands for **Social Media Marketing**. An SMM panel is a self-service website where you buy social media engagement in bulk: followers, likes, views, subscribers, comments and members. Instead of hiring a freelancer, you select a ready-made service, enter your profile or post link, choose the quantity and pay from your wallet balance.

The panel sends your order to the right service provider through an API, keeps checking its progress, and updates your order history automatically. People use SMM panels for **social proof** and early momentum: a new reel, video or channel looks far more trustworthy to first-time visitors when it already has some activity.

## 📘 SMM Terms Explained

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Start Time** | How long before delivery begins after you place the order. |
| **Speed** | How many units are delivered per day once the order starts. |
| **Refill** | If the count drops within the refill period, you can request a top-up. |
| **Non-Drop / Low-Drop** | How stable the service has been historically. |
| **Drip-Feed** | One large order split into smaller runs for gradual, natural growth. |
| **Partial** | Only part of the quantity was delivered. The rest is refunded to your wallet. |

## 🔐 Is It Safe to Use an SMM Panel?

Followers India **never asks for your social media password**. You only share a public link. For the best results:

- Keep your account **public** until the order completes
- Do not change your username during an active order
- Avoid placing two orders on the same link at the same time
- Choose **refill-backed** or **drip-feed** services for followers
- Start with a **small test order**, then scale up

Note that social platforms remove inactive accounts on their own schedule and their terms discourage buying engagement, so some drop is always possible. That is why refill services exist.
